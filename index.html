Create your list of subjects in the Schedules page. You can also upload one from your files.

2

Once the subjects are set up, navigate to the Calendar page to mark your daily attendance. You need to visit this page daily. You can also add quick notes or introduce new subjects for any specific date as needed.

3

View attendance statistics on the Stats page. Check subject-wise percentages to monitor your progress effectively.

Use the Backup and Restore options to back up your attendance data or restore it later.

If your class schedule changes, you can modify or add new subjects for specific days while keeping your existing data intact via the Schedules page.
