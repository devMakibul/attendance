About Attendance Tracker

Welcome to Attendance Tracker, your ultimate companion for managing college schedules and monitoring your daily attendance effortlessly.

Our Mission

I built Attendance Tracker with a single goal in mind: to help students stay on top of their academic commitments. Keeping track of classes, absences, and overall attendance percentages can be stressful. I simplify this process by offering a clean, intuitive, and centralized dashboard.

Key Features

  • Cloud Synchronization: Seamlessly back up and sync your attendance data across all your devices using your secure Google or Email account.
  • Interactive Calendar: A beautiful visual calendar to mark your daily presence or absence efficiently.
  • Detailed Statistics: Track your attendance percentages per subject and overall, ensuring you meet your academic requirements.
  • Attendance Goals: Instantly know the exact number of classes you need to attend to reach your target percentage, or how many you can safely afford to miss.

Get in Touch

Attendance Tracker is actively maintained and improved. We'd love to hear your feedback, feature requests, or bug reports. Feel free to contact the developer directly.
