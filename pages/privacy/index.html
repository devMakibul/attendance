Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, used, and safeguarded when using the Attendance Tracker service.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your Name, Email Address, and Authentication Tokens when you choose to register an account (either via Email/Password or through Third-Party SSO like Google). We also collect user-generated data, which includes your class schedules and daily attendance records.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used strictly for providing and improving the Attendance Tracker features. Authentication tokens are used to keep you securely signed in. Your attendance records are securely synced to your cloud account to allow access across devices and prevent data loss.

3. Local Storage and Cookies

We utilize the browser's Local Storage to cache data for offline use and speed up load times. We may use cookies primarily for session authentication purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Our backend and database infrastructure is powered by Google Firebase. Firebase securely handles the storage, token validation, and delivery of your data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to outside marketing or advertising agencies.

5. Data Deletion

You reserve the right to request deletion of your data at any time. By terminating your account, all associated records, configurations, and personal identifiers held on our servers are permanently removed.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our practices or service offerings. Continuous use of our platform constitutes acceptance of those changes.
